--- title: "Content Provenance and Authenticity" description: "Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) is an open standard that provides a framework for embedding secure, tamper-evident metadata into digital media to prove its origin, authenticity, and any edits made" ---

ImageMagick integrates the C2PA coder to verify content provenance. This enables trusted workflows for digital assets.

Decoding Example

To inspect and verify the embedded C2PA data, run: 

magick identify c2pa:signed_image.png

Conclusion

The C2PA coder extends ImageMagick’s capabilities by allowing developers to decode provenance metadata alongside standard image processing tasks.
