Sponsor the ImageMagick Project

ImageMagick is one of the most trusted open-source image processing tools in the world. Free to use without restriction, it powers thousands of enterprise websites, Hollywood movie studios, publishing platforms, and everyday apps.

Unlike abandoned open-source projects, ImageMagick is actively maintained and continuously improved. If your business relies on our engine, consider giving back. Your contribution to ImageMagick Studio LLC ensures the software stays fast, reliable, secure, and free for everyone.

Ways You Can Support ImageMagick
  • Contribute source code or patches to help improve ImageMagick.
  • Send us a licensed copy of Adobe Photoshop.
  • Lend developers for additional formats or SVG support.
  • Extend our FFT algorithm to support non-square images.
  • Port our Linux build environment from Autoconf to CMake.
  • Provide technical writers for commercial-grade documentation.
  • Donate a quality SWOP or Eurostandard ICC profile.
  • Donate a workstation with modern processor and memory.
  • Donate a Windows laptop for Windows 11 testing.
  • Make a monetary contribution (anonymous or public).
